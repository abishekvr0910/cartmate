# CartMate moderated usability rehearsal

**Status: prepared, not conducted.** No real humans have tested this candidate in this checkout. Recruit only after the founder approves a safe testing arrangement; do not confuse a mock/local session with admission to a live service. Do not ask participants to dial 112, share identity documents, or provide a real emergency contact during a rehearsal.

## Set-up

Use a fresh disposable test database or a controlled staging environment approved for testing. Give each participant a realistic but fictional task in Polish. Ask permission before taking notes or recording; do not collect partner/employer rosters or reveal other participants' private activity. Arrange a facilitator and a separate observer. Include people using different phone sizes and, if available, people who use keyboard or assistive technology. Decide the participant mix and consent/retention process with the founder and privacy reviewer before recruitment.

## Tasks to observe without coaching

1. **Operator Admission & 18+ Pending State:** From the login screen, open the "🔑 Aktywuj" tab, enter a supplied `CMACT-...` pilot token, fill name and city (Katowice). Observe the transition to the 18+ Pending screen: generate the 5-minute challenge code. Describe whether the participant understands why live outings are paused pending witnessed verification.
2. **Account Recovery Test:** Log out or simulate clearing local storage, navigate to "🔑 Aktywuj", switch to "Odzyskaj konto", enter a single-use `CMREC-...` recovery token, and observe whether the original account ID and name are restored cleanly.
3. **Plan Discovery & Evaluation:** From the main feed, locate a published outing and explain *where, when, expected duration, and cost-sharing rule* before requesting to join.
4. **Join & Mutual Coordination:** Send a join request. Confirm that the UI reports "Oczekuje na akceptację". After host acceptance, mutually accept the meeting terms, perform the 4-digit PIN verification handshake at rendezvous, and add a shared expense item to verify 50/50 balance settlement (e.g. 40.00 zł total, 20.00 zł net).
5. **Low-Guilt Cancellation:** Cancel an active outing. Observe what both participants see, whether the cancellation banner is reassuring and unambiguous, and whether the participant feels judged or penalized.
6. **Safety, Blocking & Emergency Guidance:** Locate how to report a message, block a user, and access safety information. Observe understanding that SOS is direct voice `tel:112` and that CartMate does not monitor chat or dispatch emergency responders.
7. **Network Interruption & Error Handling:** Simulate a lost connection or server restart. Observe whether reconnection state is clearly displayed, drafts are retained, and duplicate messages are avoided.

Record task completion, wrong turns, time-to-understand, exact confusing copy, comfort/safety concerns, and whether participants correctly understood verification and emergency limitations. Ask an open final question: "What would stop you using this for a real short public outing?" Do not optimize for time in feed or streaks. Review severe misunderstandings and safety concerns before any invitation batch; retest changed screens.
