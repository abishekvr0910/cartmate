## Definition of Done

A ticket is done only when its behavior is implemented, automated tests cover both success and denial/failure cases, lint/typecheck/build pass, privacy and authorization effects are reviewed, and the result is demonstrated in staging where the ticket touches a provider or deployment. A passing compile or a manually cleared in-memory test is not sufficient evidence.

## Market patterns adopted as principles

- **BlaBlaCar:** keep profiles, plans, shared expectations, and cancellation understandable before people commit; CartMate's mutual plan acceptance and low-guilt cancellation remain the product-specific implementation.
- **Timeleft:** make joining a small offline social activity structured and low-friction; keep preferences opt-in and avoid exposing raw answers or inventing compatibility scores.
- **Discourse:** route reports to a human-only review queue, retain decision notes and actor attribution, and prevent ordinary users from reaching moderator actions. CartMate uses oldest-first triage rather than trusting user-selected categories as automatic severity.
- **Noonlight:** treat monitored dispatch as a real staffed service, not a button or marketing claim. CartMate must keep its direct 112 fallback until a provider confirms Polish coverage, operating procedures, and a tested integration.

References: [BlaBlaCar trust and safety](https://www.blablacar.pl/carpool/trust-safety), [Timeleft matching](https://help.timeleft.com/hc/en-150/articles/28530361837212-How-Does-Timeleft-Match-Me-With-a-Group), [Discourse review queue](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-moderation-guide-part-3-managing-content/406266), [Noonlight personal-safety dispatch](https://www.noonlight.com/solutions/personal-safety).

## Release gates

1. **Security boundary:** no fabricated identity, demo credential, self-approved ID check, or ordinary-user moderation power; every sensitive endpoint has owner/role tests.
2. **Core journey:** a real user can authenticate, discover, request/join, explicitly agree to accurate plan terms, coordinate, cancel, report/block, and log out without fake success states.
3. **Safety truth:** SOS, location, verification, and social-profile language describes only verified behavior; moderation has a named human owner and escalation process.
4. **Data and operations:** production configuration fails closed, migrations and persistence are tested, backups can be restored, provider readiness is demonstrated, logs are redacted, and rollback/runbooks exist.
5. **Quality:** isolated backend tests, frontend user-journey tests, accessibility checks, and production build all pass in CI.
6. **Pilot:** only after gates 1–5, invite a small Silesia cohort, observe, fix, then decide whether to expand.
