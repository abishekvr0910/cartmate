# Project overview

**Current hosting decision (2026-10-13):** The founder has authorized a paid **single-instance EC2 staging host** for the closed Silesia pilot; see [the EC2 runbook](../ops/EC2_STAGING_RUNBOOK.md). The older zero-new-paid-services and local-Tailscale hosting statements below are historical constraints superseded **only for the host**. SMS remains disabled, admission/adult checks remain mandatory, and no public launch is approved. No EC2 deployment has yet been verified.

CartMate helps adults find company for ordinary life: a walk, study session, workout, meal, conversation or errand. It turns a clear, short activity into a mutually agreed outing with a real person. Connection is the aim; shopping is one possible reason to leave home, not the product's boundary. It emphasizes low-guilt cancellation, specific public meeting points and transparent spending terms; it is not a courier gig or dating marketplace.

The intended first real use is a **two-adult, invite-only Silesia friend test** with **zero new paid services**. On 2026-10-12 the founder approved a private **Android test APK as the next milestone**, reusing the existing website/backend; iPhone is later. See feature 002. No wider Poland meetup launch is approved. A Poland-wide information page is a separate future product decision, not a live-outing expansion.

Primary journey: an admitted adult opens the private site, authenticates truthfully, discovers or creates a local outing, requests and accepts a companion, agrees on the activity/time/duration/cost plan, chats, meets at a public point, and can cancel, block, report or call 112. Every live action must remain server-authorized.

Pilot success is not a test count: two consenting adults use the final private HTTPS site on their own phones, complete the journey without fabricated account/age claims, understand the safety limits, and report whether the experience is actually useful. Record failures and confusing moments. The computer hosting it must remain on during staffed test hours; this is not 24/7 service.

Out of scope: paid SMS or hosting, public signups, national live meetups, app-store publishing/iPhone packaging, payments, autonomous Jev decisions, women-only verification, emergency SMS dispatch and claims of independent certification without external review. Private Android packaging is now in scope through feature 002; it does not waive feature 001's safety gates.
